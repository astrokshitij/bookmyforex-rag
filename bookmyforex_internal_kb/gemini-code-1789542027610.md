# 1. Emergency Procedures (Loss, Theft, Stuck ATMs, Failed Dispensation)
* **Card Stuck in Foreign ATM:** Wait 2–3 minutes, check ATM error messages, contact the ATM-owning bank immediately, lock the card via the BookMyForex app, and email `bmfyblcard@bookmyforex.com` to block it permanently if unrecoverable[cite: 3].
* **ATM Debited But Cash Not Dispensed:** Do not retry immediately. Keep the receipt, check card balance via app, notify the ATM-owning bank, and email `bmfyblcard@bookmyforex.com` with transaction details to raise a chargeback dispute[cite: 3].
* **Lost or Stolen Card Abroad:** Immediately lock via app or email `bmfyblcard@bookmyforex.com`. Report the loss to local police (obtain FIR/acknowledgment copy) and request a replacement card[cite: 3].
* **Unauthorized Transactions (ECOM/ATM/POS):** Immediately lock/block the card, report details to support, complete a dispute declaration form, and initiate a chargeback investigation (resolution timeline: up to 90 days)[cite: 3].

# 2. PIN Management & Blocks
* **Incorrect PIN Block:** After **3 consecutive incorrect ATM PIN attempts**, the card is temporarily blocked for ATM/POS transactions for **24 hours** (automatically lifts after 24 hours)[cite: 3]. It is recommended to reset the PIN via the app before reuse[cite: 3].

# 3. Comprehensive Insurance Claims (Up to ₹1,50,000)
* **Coverage Scope:** Protects primary cardholders against unauthorized international transactions outside India resulting from skimming, phishing, counterfeit/duplicate cards, and online fraud (claims must be reported within 30 days of the first fraudulent transaction)[cite: 3].
* **Exclusions:** Family/insider fraud, bank system failures, domestic Indian transactions, and terrorism-related losses[cite: 3].
* **Mandatory Claim Documents:**
  1. Duly filled and signed Insurance Claim Form[cite: 3].
  2. **Police Acknowledgment or FIR** (Online FIR is **not** accepted)[cite: 3].
  3. Formal Dispute Letter[cite: 3].
  4. Card statement covering 3 months prior to the disputed transaction up to the last transaction date[cite: 3].
  5. Complete passport copy including all immigration stamp pages[cite: 3].

---

# 4. Avoiding Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC)
* **What it is:** ATMs/merchants offering to charge transactions in your home currency (INR) instead of local foreign currency using unfavorable exchange rates[cite: 3].
* **Resolution:** Always choose to **pay in the local currency** of the destination country and decline DCC prompts to prevent hidden conversion fees[cite: 3].
