# Currency Exchange / Currency Notes

## What the service does
BookMyForex facilitates purchase and sale of foreign currency through its online platform and authorised channel/partner network. Currency availability varies by location and inventory.

## Typical customer flow
1. Select city/location, currency, buy/sell requirement and amount.
2. Review the displayed rate/product availability.
3. Choose fixed-rate/rate-freeze or applicable booking option.
4. Provide KYC/travel documents.
5. Complete payment as required.
6. Receive currency by eligible doorstep delivery or pickup route.

## Common travel documents
Public location FAQs describe holiday-travel documentation as typically including:
- Passport
- Air ticket
- Visa where applicable
- PAN
Requirements can differ by transaction purpose and regulation.

## Delivery
Public pages advertise same-day currency delivery in select cities/PIN codes when the order, payment and required documents are completed before the applicable cut-off (commonly 1 PM). Otherwise delivery may move to the next business day. Holidays/location/product availability can affect this.

## Rate Freeze
Public currency-exchange FAQs describe a rate-freeze mechanism allowing a customer to lock a quoted rate for up to 3 days using a refundable deposit. One public page describes the deposit as 2%. Verify live terms before quoting because booking/deposit rules can change.

## Rates
Cash/currency-note pricing differs from forex-card and wire-transfer pricing because these are separate products. Physical currency has inventory, logistics and handling costs. Do not use a remittance or card rate when answering a cash-rate question.

## Taxes and service fees
Currency conversion is subject to applicable GST and, where applicable, TCS under LRS. Service/convenience fees can also apply. See `fees-and-charges.md` and `tcs-and-regulations.md`.

## Important support notes
- Currency/denomination availability is not guaranteed.
- Customer claims after delivery/pickup can be restricted by applicable terms.
- Never promise same-day delivery without checking location, cut-off, payment and document completion.
- Live exchange rates should be fetched from the current website/app rather than stored in RAG as durable facts.
