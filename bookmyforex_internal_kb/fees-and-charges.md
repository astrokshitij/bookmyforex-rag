# Fees & Charges

> **Time-sensitive. Verify against current product page before quoting to a customer.**

## International Money Transfer
Current public money-transfer fee page lists:
- Bank fee: ₹320 + GST
- Nostro/intermediary charge estimate: ₹1,000–₹1,500 depending on currency, partner/vendor, location and purpose.
- Recipient/intermediary banks may levy charges outside BookMyForex's direct control.
- Promotional offers can waive/reduce certain fees.

## GST on currency conversion
Public fee pages state:
- Up to ₹1 lakh: 0.18% of amount exchanged, minimum ₹45, maximum ₹180.
- Above ₹1 lakh to ₹10 lakh: ₹180 + 0.09% of amount over the applicable basis shown by the site; published maximum ₹990.
- Above ₹10 lakh: ₹990 + 0.018%, published maximum ₹10,800.
- Commission/fees/charges: 18% GST where applicable.

Use the checkout/system calculation as authoritative for a live order.

## Retail service charge — SOURCE CONFLICT
Public BookMyForex pages are not fully consistent:
- General FAQ / card FAQ: ₹300 up to ₹15k; ₹250 for ₹15,001–₹50k; ₹150 for ₹50,001–₹1 lakh; ₹100 above ₹1 lakh.
- Current Terms of Use page surfaced in search: ₹300; ₹200; ₹150; ₹100 for those same slabs.
Because of this discrepancy, **do not hard-quote the ₹15,001–₹50,000 slab from RAG. Verify current checkout/official fee page.**
