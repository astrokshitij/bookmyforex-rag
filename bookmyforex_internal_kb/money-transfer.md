# International Money Transfer

## Product overview
BookMyForex provides online outward international money transfers from India through RBI-authorised banking partners. Public pages advertise online/app booking, rate lock-in and transfers to 150+ countries.

## Standard process
1. Select destination currency and amount.
2. Review live remittance rate and applicable charges.
3. Lock the rate where eligible (public page: up to 3 working days).
4. Enter remitter/KYC details.
5. Add beneficiary bank details.
6. Upload mandatory and purpose-specific documents.
7. Complete payment.
8. Transfer is routed through an authorised partner bank/SWIFT route and tracked to the beneficiary.

## Mandatory documents
Public money-transfer pages list:
- PAN card
- KYC/address proof: Aadhaar, passport or voter ID
- A2 cum LRS declaration

## Purpose-specific documents
Additional evidence depends on purpose. Examples:
- Education: admission/university offer letter; student's passport where relevant.
- Family maintenance: relationship/purpose evidence may be requested.
- Medical: hospital/medical invoice or supporting document.
- Other purposes can require investment, emigration, employment or other supporting documents.

## Beneficiary details
Requirements vary by country:
- USA: account number + ABA routing number
- UK: sort code + account number
- Canada: transit number + institution number + account number + SWIFT
- Europe/many other destinations: IBAN + SWIFT/BIC
The beneficiary's full name/address and bank details are also required as applicable.

## Permitted purposes
Public pages list LRS-permitted purposes such as:
- Higher education/tuition
- Maintenance of close relatives
- Medical treatment
- Employment/emigration
- International travel/extended stays
- Gifts/donations
- Investments and other permitted purposes
Purpose eligibility and documentation must be validated at booking.

## Transfer time
Public pages generally state 12–48 hours after verification/payment, with some education transfers potentially processed the same day/as fast as about 6 hours. This is not a universal guarantee; destination, banking cut-offs, compliance review and correspondent banks can affect timing.

## LRS
Public pages state a resident individual can remit up to USD 250,000 equivalent per financial year across permitted LRS purposes, subject to RBI rules. Transfers above the applicable limit require the relevant approval/rule treatment.

## NRI note
Public FAQ distinguishes LRS for resident individuals from NRI repatriation. NRE/NRO repatriation follows separate rules and should not be answered using resident-LRS logic.

## Safety
Public pages state transfers use secure/encrypted connections and RBI-authorised partner banks, and payment details are not stored on BookMyForex servers.

## Fees
Current public fee page lists ₹320 + GST bank fee plus estimated Nostro/intermediary charges of roughly ₹1,000–₹1,500 depending on currency/vendor/location/purpose. Promotions may alter these. See `fees-and-charges.md`.
