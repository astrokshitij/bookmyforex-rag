# International Money Transfer

## Product overview
BookMyForex provides online outward international money transfers from India through RBI-authorised banking partners. Public pages advertise online/app booking, rate lock-in and transfers to 150+ countries.

## Standard process
1. Select destination currency and amount.
2. Review live remittance rate and applicable charges.
3. Lock the rate where eligible (public page: up to 3 working days).
4. Enter remitter/KYC details.
5. Add beneficiary bank details.
6. Upload mandatory and purpose-specific documents.
7. Complete payment.
8. Transfer is routed through an authorised partner bank/SWIFT route and tracked to the beneficiary.

## Mandatory documents
Public money-transfer pages list:
- PAN card
- KYC/address proof: Aadhaar, passport or voter ID
- A2 cum LRS declaration

## Purpose-specific documents
Additional evidence depends on purpose. Examples:
- Education: admission/university offer letter; student's passport where relevant.
- Family maintenance: relationship/purpose evidence may be requested.
- Medical: hospital/medical invoice or supporting document.
- Other purposes can require investment, emigration, employment or other supporting documents.

## Beneficiary details
Requirements vary by country:
- USA: account number + ABA routing number
- UK: sort code + account number
- Canada: transit number + institution number + account number + SWIFT
- Europe/many other destinations: IBAN + SWIFT/BIC
The beneficiary's full name/address and bank details are also required as applicable.

## RBI Purpose Codes for International Money Transfer
Under RBI FEMA guidelines, every outward international remittance must be tagged with a mandatory RBI Purpose Code number based on the nature and objective of the transfer:

### 1. Education Abroad
- **`S0305`**: Travel for education (including student hostel expenses, living costs, university fees, GIC account deposits for Canada/Germany blocked accounts).
- **`S1107`**: Remittance towards education / tuition fees paid directly to overseas educational institutes, schools, colleges, or universities.

### 2. Family Maintenance & Living Expenses
- **`S1302`**: Remittance towards maintenance of close relatives residing abroad (family maintenance, living support for spouse, parents, children, or siblings).

### 3. Medical Treatment Abroad
- **`S0304`**: Travel for medical treatment abroad (patient travel and accompanying attendant expenses).
- **`S1108`**: Remittance towards medical expenses and treatment costs paid directly to overseas hospitals, doctors, or healthcare clinics.

### 4. Personal Gifts & Donations
- **`S1301`**: Remittance towards personal gifts and donations to individuals or charitable/educational institutions abroad.

### 5. Private Travel & Tourism
- **`S0306`**: Other personal travel / leisure / holiday / vacation expenses abroad under LRS.
- **`S0301`**: Business travel expenses (meetings, conferences, trade exhibitions).

### 6. Emigration & Employment
- **`S0303`**: Travel for employment abroad.
- **`S1303`**: Remittance for emigration and emigration consultancy fees / settlement costs.

### 7. Trade, Commercial & Business Services (Import of Goods/Services)
- **`S0101`**: Advance payment against import of physical goods.
- **`S0102`**: Payment against import of physical goods / invoice settlement.
- **`S0103`**: Payment for imports through Letter of Credit (LC) / document collection.
- **`S1002`**: Payment for software purchase, cloud hosting, SaaS subscriptions, and digital IT services.
- **`S1007`**: Advertising, PR, digital marketing, and promotional service fees.
- **`S1008`**: Legal, accounting, taxation, auditing, and business management consultancy fees.

### 8. Overseas Investments & Property (under LRS)
- **`S0001` / `S0003`**: Overseas direct investment (ODI) / investment in foreign equity shares, mutual funds, or securities.
- **`S0005`**: Remittance for acquisition / purchase of immovable property (real estate) abroad.

## Permitted purposes
Public pages list LRS-permitted purposes such as:
- Higher education/tuition (`S0305`, `S1107`)
- Maintenance of close relatives (`S1302`)
- Medical treatment (`S0304`, `S1108`)
- Employment/emigration (`S0303`, `S1303`)
- International travel/extended stays (`S0306`, `S0301`)
- Gifts/donations (`S1301`)
- Commercial trade & service remittances (`S0101`, `S0102`, `S1002`, `S1008`)
- Investments and other permitted purposes
Purpose eligibility, purpose code selection, and supporting documentation must be validated at booking.

## Transfer time
Public pages generally state 12–48 hours after verification/payment, with some education transfers potentially processed the same day/as fast as about 6 hours. This is not a universal guarantee; destination, banking cut-offs, compliance review and correspondent banks can affect timing.

## LRS
Public pages state a resident individual can remit up to USD 250,000 equivalent per financial year across permitted LRS purposes, subject to RBI rules. Transfers above the applicable limit require the relevant approval/rule treatment.

## NRI note
Public FAQ distinguishes LRS for resident individuals from NRI repatriation. NRE/NRO repatriation follows separate rules and should not be answered using resident-LRS logic.

## Safety
Public pages state transfers use secure/encrypted connections and RBI-authorised partner banks, and payment details are not stored on BookMyForex servers.

## Fees
Current public fee page lists ₹320 + GST bank fee plus estimated Nostro/intermediary charges of roughly ₹1,000–₹1,500 depending on currency/vendor/location/purpose. Promotions may alter these. See `fees-and-charges.md`.
