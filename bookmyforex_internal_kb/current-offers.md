## Cashback Terms & Conditions and MyCash Payout Policy
All BookMyForex promotional cashbacks are subject to the following mandatory payout terms and conditions:
- **Bookings done till 15th September:** Cashback will be credited directly to the respective bank account used for making successful payment on the BookMyForex platform.
- **Bookings done from 16th September onwards:** Cashback will be credited as **MyCash** in the customer's **MakeMyTrip account**.
- **What is MyCash / How to use MyCash:**
  - MyCash can be used completely (100%) on booking flights, hotels, and holidays on MakeMyTrip.
  - In case of buses and cabs, the minimum booking amount should be ₹500 to be able to use MyCash completely.
  - **Accessing MyCash:** Visit the MakeMyTrip app or website and log in with the email or mobile number used for making this booking. Check MyCash balance and detailed terms at [makemytrip.com/myWallet](http://makemytrip.com/myWallet).
- **Recipient:** Cashback is credited only to the customer who placed the order.
- **Timeline:** Cashback is generally credited within 30 days of successful order/transfer completion, subject to offer-specific verification.
