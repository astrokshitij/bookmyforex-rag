# FAQ & Support Playbook

## Customer asks: What documents do I need for a Forex Card?
Passport is mandatory; multi-currency card issuance also requires valid PAN linked with Aadhaar. Travel-related proof is required. Exact documentation can vary by purpose/order.

## Customer asks: Can an NRI/foreigner get the Forex Card?
Public card FAQ says no for the BookMyForex Forex Card flow.

## Customer asks: How quickly will my Forex Card arrive?
Public pages advertise same-day delivery in select cities subject to cut-off, payment, documents and working-day/holiday rules; courier delivery is also available. Do not promise without checking PIN/location.

## Customer asks: How do I reload?
Use the BookMyForex app. Link the Yes Bank multi-currency card first, then use Add Money/reload flow.

## Customer asks: Can I use the card in a currency I didn't load?
Yes, but cross-currency conversion/fees may apply depending on card variant. Check Schedule of Charges.

## Customer asks: Should I accept INR/home-currency conversion at an overseas ATM/POS?
Generally no. Public terms warn against Dynamic Currency Conversion; transact in the local currency to avoid adverse conversion.

## Customer asks: What documents are mandatory for international money transfer?
PAN + KYC/address proof + A2 cum LRS declaration, plus purpose-specific documents.

## Customer asks: How long does an international transfer take?
Public pages state most transfers around 12–48 hours after verification/payment; some education transfers can be faster. Never guarantee timing because compliance/banks/destination can affect it.

## Customer asks: What are money-transfer charges?
Current public fee page: ₹320 + GST bank fee plus estimated Nostro/intermediary charges of ₹1,000–₹1,500, subject to currency/vendor/purpose and offers.

## Customer asks: Can I cancel?
Public FAQ says unpaid/no-deposit orders can generally be cancelled without charge; forfeiture/cancellation fees can apply once a rate-guarantee deposit or full payment has been made. Verify the current cancellation/refund terms for the specific product/order.

## Customer asks: What is Pay on Delivery?
Public FAQ describes it as an option under a no-obligation order where payment is not made until the forex product is received, where available.

## Customer asks: Is same-day delivery guaranteed?
No universal guarantee. It depends on product, city/PIN, working day/holiday, order cut-off, successful payment and complete documents.

## Escalate when
- Customer disputes a rate/charge already applied
- KYC/residency is unclear
- Transaction exceeds/approaches regulatory limits
- Compliance/AML flag
- Beneficiary bank rejects/returns transfer
- Offer eligibility is unclear
- Website pages show conflicting charges
- Refund/cancellation has already entered processing
