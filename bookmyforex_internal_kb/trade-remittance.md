# Trade & Commercial Remittance

## Product overview
BookMyForex markets a separate trade-remittance service for commercial/cross-border payments to foreign businesses and trading partners.

## Publicly advertised capabilities
- Online/paperless process
- No routine bank-branch visit
- Live and transparent trade rates on a personalised dashboard
- Rate alerts
- Dedicated trade/foreign-remittance assistance
- SPOT and forward-rate booking
- Public page states rates can be locked from 3 days up to 1 year, depending on product/eligibility.

## Typical use
Commercial payments and payments to overseas trading partners. The trade-remittance flow should not be confused with personal LRS remittance; business transactions can have different FEMA, documentation, purpose-code and compliance requirements.

## Internal support rule
For commercial transactions, collect the organisation/individual details, location, import/export context and expected volume, then route to the Trade & Foreign Remittances team rather than applying retail LRS guidance.
