# Trade & Commercial Remittance

## Product overview
BookMyForex markets a separate trade-remittance service for commercial/cross-border payments to foreign businesses and trading partners.

## Publicly advertised capabilities
- Online/paperless process
- No routine bank-branch visit
- Live and transparent trade rates on a personalised dashboard
- Rate alerts
- Dedicated trade/foreign-remittance assistance
- SPOT and forward-rate booking
- Public page states rates can be locked from 3 days up to 1 year, depending on product/eligibility.

## RBI Purpose Codes for Trade & Commercial Remittance
Commercial remittances require specific FEMA trade purpose code numbers based on the underlying commercial invoice/contract:
- **`S0101`**: Advance payment against import of physical goods.
- **`S0102`**: Payment against physical goods imports / bill of entry settlement.
- **`S0103`**: Payment for imports under Letter of Credit (LC) / documentary collection.
- **`S1002`**: Payment for software licenses, digital IT services, cloud hosting, and SaaS subscriptions.
- **`S1007`**: Advertising, digital marketing, PR, and market research service fees.
- **`S1008`**: Legal fees, accounting, audit, management consulting, and advisory services.
- **`S1006`**: Architectural, engineering, and technical service fees.

## Typical use
Commercial payments and payments to overseas trading partners. The trade-remittance flow should not be confused with personal LRS remittance; business transactions can have different FEMA, documentation, purpose-code and compliance requirements.

## Internal support rule
For commercial transactions, collect the organisation/individual details, location, import/export context, purpose code, and expected volume, then route to the Trade & Foreign Remittances team rather than applying retail LRS guidance.
