# Forex Card

## Product overview
BookMyForex offers prepaid travel forex cards for eligible international travellers. Public pages describe a Yes Bank co-branded multi-currency card and a single-currency card issued by Nium; some locations may occasionally have an ICICI multi-currency card. Availability can vary by location and time.

## Card variants
### Multi-currency Forex Card
- Co-branded with Yes Bank and Visa-powered.
- Supports multiple foreign-currency wallets.
- Can be reloaded through the BookMyForex app after linking the card.
- App controls include card lock/unlock, ATM PIN reset, payment-channel controls and configurable spending limits.
- Usage-based charges may apply, including ATM, cash-disbursal and cross-currency charges.

### Single-currency Forex Card
- Public FAQ describes it as issued by Nium.
- Positioned particularly for students/travellers.
- Public pages state zero cross-currency and zero cash-disbursal fees for this variant; verify current schedule before quoting.

## Eligibility / documentation
- Passport is mandatory.
- For the multi-currency card, PAN must be linked with Aadhaar.
- Card is for customers travelling abroad; travel proof is checked.
- Public FAQ says foreigners and NRIs cannot obtain the card through BookMyForex.
- Public FAQ states forex can generally be purchased within 60 days before travel, subject to RBI rules.

## Delivery and activation
- Cards can be couriered across India; same-working-day delivery is advertised in select cities.
- Same-day service is subject to order timing, payment, documents, location and holidays.
- Public FAQ says the card is loaded/activated within up to 6 working hours after required payment/documents are received.
- Activation confirmation is sent by email/SMS.

## Reloading
Use the BookMyForex mobile app. Yes Bank multi-currency cards must first be linked in the app. Reload processing is stated as up to 6 working hours after payment and documents, subject to exceptions.

## Usage
- Merchant POS/e-commerce transactions generally do not have a separate transaction fee, but cross-currency conversion can apply.
- Transactions in a currency not loaded/denominated on the card can trigger network conversion and cross-currency fees.
- Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC) should generally be declined when the merchant/ATM offers to charge in the cardholder's 'home' currency; use the local transaction currency instead.
- Certain pre-authorisation/temporary-hold transactions may be disabled on the Yes Bank multi-currency card, including some hotel/car-rental deposits.
- Spending limits depend on card/currency and can be reduced via the app.
- Disposable virtual cards can be generated for e-commerce; the public FAQ says they can be valid for up to 10 days.

## Replacement
Public FAQ states multi-currency replacement-card fees of ₹250 in India and ₹3,000 outside India. **Verify current Schedule of Charges before quoting.**

## Zero-markup conditions
The public Terms of Use states the advertised zero-markup/interbank-rate offer is conditional. It is described for the BookMyForex–Yes Bank card, selected currencies, eligible cities/order values, banking/market hours and promo conditions. It does not automatically apply to cross-currency transactions, unloading/refunds or every order. Always verify the current offer/T&C.

## Regulatory limit
Public FAQ references the RBI LRS aggregate limit of USD 250,000 equivalent per resident individual per financial year across applicable forex transactions/purposes.
