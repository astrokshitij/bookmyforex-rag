# TCS, LRS & Regulatory Notes

> **Regulatory/tax information is time-sensitive. Snapshot: 18 September 2026. Verify before customer-facing advice.**

## TCS from 1 April 2026 — public BookMyForex guidance
BookMyForex's current TCS pages state an annual LRS threshold of ₹10 lakh.

### Purchase of forex / Forex Card
- Public calculator states 20% TCS on applicable forex purchase amount above ₹10 lakh in the financial year, subject to purpose/rules.

### Outward remittance — education financed by eligible education loan
- Public money-transfer fee page states Nil TCS.

### Self-funded education / medical treatment
- Nil up to ₹10 lakh annual threshold.
- 2% on the applicable amount above ₹10 lakh, per current BookMyForex 2026 guidance.

### Other LRS purposes
- Nil up to ₹10 lakh.
- 20% on the applicable excess above ₹10 lakh.

## Important
TCS is a collection mechanism, not necessarily the customer's final income-tax liability; BookMyForex's public guidance notes it may be claimed as tax credit subject to tax rules.

## LRS annual limit
Public BookMyForex FAQs cite USD 250,000 equivalent per resident individual per financial year across permitted LRS purposes, subject to RBI/FEMA rules.

## Compliance
BookMyForex Terms reference compliance with applicable FEMA/RBI rules, AML requirements, PMLA, Income Tax rules and other applicable laws.

## Support guardrails
- Never calculate regulatory liability from memory when checkout/system data is available.
- Ask for purpose, financial-year aggregate and funding type where relevant.
- Distinguish resident LRS remittance from NRI NRE/NRO repatriation.
- Escalate ambiguous residency, source-of-funds, sanctions/AML, corporate-remittance or regulatory cases to compliance/operations.
