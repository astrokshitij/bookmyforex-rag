# Terms, Policies & Operational Caveats

## Fixed-rate vs no-obligation orders
BookMyForex terms distinguish booking structures including fixed-rate/rate-lock and no-obligation arrangements. Cancellation/refund consequences depend on whether a deposit/full payment has been made and the product/order state.

## Cancellation
Public FAQ states:
- Orders with neither full payment nor refundable rate-guarantee deposit can generally be cancelled without charge.
- If a rate-guarantee deposit was paid, cancellation can result in forfeiture of that deposit.
- If full payment has been received, the public FAQ describes refund after a cancellation fee of 2% of order total.
Always verify current product/order terms before committing.

## Payment convenience fee
Public Terms state convenience fees, once paid, are non-refundable even if an order is cancelled. Current payment-method amounts should be verified.

## Forex Card
Card usage is subject to issuer/card-network terms, available balance, supported transaction types, currency conversion and applicable usage fees. DCC and pre-authorisation/temporary-hold transactions have special caveats described in card terms.

## Privacy / payment security
Public FAQ states card/net-banking payments are processed over secure HTTPS/trusted gateways and payment credentials are not stored on BookMyForex servers. Privacy handling is governed by the site's Privacy Policy and applicable law.

## Regulatory compliance
Users/partners are required to comply with applicable FEMA/RBI, AML/PMLA, tax and other laws. Support staff should not override compliance/document requirements based on customer urgency.

## Insurance
BookMyForex pages state insurance is subject to solicitation and applicable IRDAI rules/insurer offer documents. BookMyForex identifies IRDAI registration CA0429 on its public footer.
