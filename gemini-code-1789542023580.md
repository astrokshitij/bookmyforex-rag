# 1. Product Overview & Eligibility
* **Issuer:** Issued in partnership with **YES Bank**[cite: 2].
* **Scope:** Allows users to load and carry up to **14 major foreign currencies** on a single prepaid card[cite: 2].
* **Eligibility Criteria:**
  * Resident Indian customers aged **14 years and above**[cite: 2].
  * Travel Purpose: **Leisure / Holiday Travel** or **Business Travel** only[cite: 2]. *(Note: Education, employment, or immigration travel must use the Single Currency NIUM/Instarem Forex Card)[cite: 2].*
  * Required Docs: Valid Indian Passport, PAN Card (mandatory even for minors), confirmed air tickets, valid visa (where applicable), and compliance with RBI/FEMA guidelines[cite: 2]. Payments must originate from the traveler's own Indian savings bank account[cite: 2].

---

# 2. Key Features & Fee Structure
* **Rates & Fees:** Fixed exchange rates at loading; **Zero** issuance, reload, unload, or annual maintenance fees[cite: 2].
* **Transaction Fees:**
  * POS (Merchant swipe/tap) & E-commerce (Online) in loaded currencies: **Free**[cite: 2].
  * Cross-Currency Transactions (unloaded currency): **3.5%** of transaction value[cite: 2].
  * ATM Cash Withdrawal Fee: Varies by currency (e.g., USD 2, EUR 1.5, GBP 1.5, AUD 2, CAD 2 per withdrawal, plus 18% GST)[cite: 2].
  * Cash Disbursement / Cash@POS: **2%** of transaction amount[cite: 2].
* **Security & App Controls:** Chip & PIN protected, instant card lock/unlock, PIN reset, channel controls (ATM, POS, E-commerce, Contactless), and spending limits managed via the BookMyForex App[cite: 2].

---

# 3. Operational Processes
* **Purchase & Delivery:** Order via website, app, or MakeMyTrip. Same-day doorstep delivery available for eligible orders placed before cut-off times. Original passport required for live verification upon delivery[cite: 2].
* **Reload Process:** Reload via App/Website using UPI/Net Banking from the cardholder's own account. 
  * *Current Trip:* Instant reload (processed within 10 minutes)[cite: 2].
  * *New Trip:* Processed within 6 working hours post document verification[cite: 2].
* **Unload Process:** Request unload via App/Website. Balance converted to INR and credited to the registered Indian bank account within **5 working days**[cite: 2].
* **Replacement Card:** Request via App under "Manage Your Card". Delivered within 5 working days in India (₹250 fee) or 10 working days internationally (₹3,000 fee)[cite: 2].

---

# 4. Virtual Forex Card Feature
* **What it is:** A free, disposable digital proxy card generated via the BookMyForex app sharing the physical card's balance[cite: 2].
* **Usage & Security:** Valid for **10 days**, designed exclusively for online/e-commerce transactions to protect physical card details from merchant fraud[cite: 2]. Cannot be used for ATM withdrawals or POS swipes[cite: 2].
