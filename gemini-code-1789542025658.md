# 1. Product Overview & Eligibility
* **Scope:** Prepaid travel card loaded strictly in **US Dollars (USD)**, accepted in 200+ countries and transactions across 180+ currencies[cite: 5].
* **Target Audience / Eligibility:** Mandatory for customers traveling for **Education, Employment, Immigration**, FATF high-risk jurisdictions, or destinations unsupported by the YES Bank multi-currency card[cite: 5].
* **Key Benefits:** Zero cross-currency conversion charges, zero card issuance/reload/unload/annual maintenance fees, and zero ATM withdrawal fees from the card issuer[cite: 5].

---

# 2. Portal Management & App Limitations
* **Important Rule:** The Instarem/Global USD card **cannot** be managed inside the BookMyForex mobile app[cite: 5]. 
* **Card Portal:** Customers must register and manage their card via the official Instarem portal (`instarem.co.in` or NIUM portal)[cite: 5].
* **Management Features:** View balances, check transaction history, reset ATM PIN, update channel controls, and set spending limits via the external portal[cite: 5].
* **Customer Support:** Email: `cards.support@instarem.com` | Phone: `+91 22 6945 6311`[cite: 5].

---

# 3. Operational Guidelines & Troubleshooting
* **Reload Rules:** Permitted only for **Education** and **Leisure/Holiday** travel purposes (strictly prohibited for Employment or Immigration reloads)[cite: 5]. Must be paid from the cardholder's own bank account[cite: 5].
* **Unload Process:** Request via BookMyForex website/app; balance converted to INR and credited to the registered Indian bank account within **5 working days** post-verification[cite: 5].
* **Hold Amounts:** Temporary reservation of funds for unsettled transactions (automatically released within 7–14 business days once settled by the merchant)[cite: 5].
* **Common Decline Reasons:** 
  * *Channel Blocked:* Transaction channel (ATM/POS/ECOM) is disabled in portal controls[cite: 5].
  * *Transaction Restricted:* Attempting domestic INR transactions in India (Forex cards are strictly for international usage)[cite: 5].
