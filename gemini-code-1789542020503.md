# 1. Company Overview & Background
* **Founded:** 2012 (India's first online foreign exchange and international payments platform)[cite: 4].
* **Headquarters:** Gurugram, India[cite: 4].
* **Core Offerings:** Transparent real-time forex rates, RBI-authorized services in partnership with leading banks, operations across 650+ cities, and trusted by 300,000+ customers[cite: 4].
* **Key Leadership:**

  * **Nitin Motwani:** Co-Founder & CTO (leads technology infrastructure and product development)[cite: 4].

# 2. Corporate History & Milestones
* **2011:** Founded with a vision to digitize foreign exchange in India; among the first online forex marketplaces[cite: 4].
* **2012:** Platform launched live for customers; introduced online forex card booking, currency exchange, and remittances[cite: 4].
* **2013-2015:** Expanded across multiple cities; introduced rate comparison, doorstep delivery, and order tracking[cite: 4].
* **2016:** Faering Capital invested, acquiring a ~26% stake to support expansion[cite: 4].
* **2022:** MakeMyTrip's fintech arm (TripMoney) acquired a majority stake, creating powerful travel industry synergies[cite: 4].
* **2024:** Released app upgrades featuring real-time card reloads and enhanced user experiences[cite: 4].
* **2025:** Achieved operational profitability and launched new Global Forex Cards targeting students and frequent travelers[cite: 4].

# 3. Products & Services
1. **Forex Cards:** Multi-currency prepaid travel cards usable abroad for payments and ATM withdrawals. Variants include Zero Markup Forex Cards (Interbank rate cards) and Global Forex Cards (ideal for students/frequent travelers)[cite: 4].
2. **Currency Exchange (Cash):** Buy or sell foreign currency notes (USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, SGD, etc.) at live rates with doorstep delivery/pickup[cite: 4].
3. **International Money Transfer (Remittance):** Outward remittances under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) for education fees, medical treatment, family maintenance, immigration, and visa fees[cite: 4].
4. **Travel Insurance:** Comprehensive international travel insurance covering medical emergencies, trip delays, and baggage loss[cite: 4].
